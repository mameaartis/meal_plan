How the app enforces it

  • One challenge food per 7-day week — so never two FODMAP groups in one week.
  • Challenge days 1–3 (optional 4) run on consecutive days inside that week.
  • Washout: at least 2 days, 3 after any symptoms. After strong symptoms you confirm they’ve settled.
  • The order follows your document and can’t be changed. Nothing advances until you review the outcome.
  • Portions are shown exactly as written. Day 4 has no amount — it’s your usual portion.
  • During a challenge week, meals with foods from another group are blocked from the planner; foods from the same group are flagged as incidental exposure.
  • Foods you’ve already tested stay flagged until reintroduction ends.
